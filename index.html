How to play

Every hex you touch, you take. Most power at the end wins.

  • Pick one of your cats and a glowing hex. Clone (gold, 1 step) adds a new cat. Leap (green, 2 steps) moves the cat and leaves its old hex empty.
  • Every enemy touching the hex you land on joins your side.
  • Merge (violet): step onto your own neighbouring cat of the same level to fuse into a crowned cat (★2, then ★3). Crowns are worth their level in points, and a hit only knocks a crown off instead of converting. Merging converts nothing itself: it's armour, and it frees a hex.
  • If the side to move can't move, the other side claims every empty hex and the game ends. With three players, a stuck player is skipped.
  • If 50 moves pass without the board getting fuller than ever before, the game ends as it stands.
  • Cascade: an enemy left touching 4+ of your cats also flips, in waves. Tectonic: every two rounds the outer ring turns. Fog: you only see near your cats. Blitz: 60 s each, +2 s a move. Misère: fewest wins, clones only.
  • 3D / 4D boards: hexes also touch the hex above and below (and their twin in the other world). 1–5 shows one layer, 0 all, X spreads the stack.

Drag a cat onto a hex, or click then click. Keys: U undo · H hint · M sound · F fullscreen · Esc menu
